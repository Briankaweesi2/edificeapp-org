A few things
you might wonder.

What is a confession?

In Edifice, a confession is a first-person declaration grounded in Scripture. Each one includes a supporting Bible passage so you can read it in context.

Can I use Edifice for free?

Yes. Free members receive 3 daily confessions. Glory members receive 20 daily confessions and access to the full confession archive.

Which Bible translation do you use?

The examples on this website use the King James Version (KJV). Scripture references are shown alongside the words.

Where can I download the app?

Edifice is available on Google Play for Android. The iPhone version is coming soon.
