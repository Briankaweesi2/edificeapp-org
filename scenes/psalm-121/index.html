Psalm 121, verse by verse

He will not slumber. The whole chapter in the King James Version, one verse to a page, each with a line to say over it. 8 verses.

Psalm 121:1

I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help.

I lift my eyes high, and my help comes from God.

Psalm 121:2

My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth.

My help comes from the Lord who created heaven and earth.

Psalm 121:3

He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber.

The Lord anchors my steps and keeps watch without ceasing.

Psalm 121:4

Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.

My Guardian never slumbers and never sleeps.

Psalm 121:5

The LORD is thy keeper: the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand.

The Lord is my keeper and my protective shadow.

Psalm 121:6

The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night.

I am shielded from harm by day and by night.

Psalm 121:7

The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.

The Lord preserves my soul and keeps me from all evil.

Psalm 121:8

The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.

The Lord preserves my journey now and forevermore.
