About Edifice

Build your life
on the Word.

Edifice helps you make space for Scripture in everyday life. It brings a confession and its supporting Bible passage together, so there is always a reference to return to.

Our aim is a simple, thoughtful practice: read the Word, speak with intention, and consider how its truth shapes the way you live.

Scripture comes first.

A confession is not a replacement for reading the Bible in context. It is a starting point for reflection. The Scripture examples on this website use the King James Version (KJV), with references shown so you can explore the passage yourself.

A rhythm you can return to.

Free members receive 3 daily confessions. Glory members receive 20 and full confession-archive access. Bible scenes offer another way to spend focused time with a passage.

Edifice is available on Android through Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon.
