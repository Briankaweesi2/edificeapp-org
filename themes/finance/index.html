Confessions for finances

Not affirmations about money. Confessions about the God who supplies it, and about the sufficiency he has already given for every good work.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

Every day is abundance for me.

And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work:

2 Corinthians 9:8 · KJV

There is no lack in my inheritance.

Giving thanks unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light

Colossians 1:12 · KJV

The Lord delights in my prosperity.

If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your trust the true riches?

Luke 16:11 · KJV

The riches of glory belong to me.

To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles; which is Christ in you, the hope of glory

Colossians 1:27 · KJV

I have no consciousness of need.

And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues; They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover

Mark 16:17-18 · KJV

I walk in continuous divine provision.

But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you

Matthew 6:33 · KJV

All things are mine in Christ.

For ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that, though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor, that ye through his poverty might be rich

2 Corinthians 8:9 · KJV

I have conquered the economy of this world.

For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith

1 John 5:4 · KJV

There is no lack in my Kingdom.

Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the kingdom

Luke 12:32 · KJV

I give according as I purpose in my heart.

Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver

2 Corinthians 9:7 · KJV

There is no poverty in my life.

And we know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true

1 John 5:20 · KJV

The lesser is included in my inheritance.

And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise

Galatians 3:29 · KJV
