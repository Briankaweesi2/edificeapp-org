Confessions on authority and dominion

Authority is exercised in words. These are confessions of what has already been given, spoken by someone who intends to use it.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

The Name of Jesus commands all evil spirits.

Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour

1 Peter 5:8 · KJV

Satan cannot rule in my domain.

Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world

1 John 4:4 · KJV

I walk in divine authority every day.

Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you

James 4:7 · KJV

I alter every negative situation.

Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much

James 5:16 · KJV

I live and function in His Name.

Know ye not that we shall judge angels? how much more things that pertain to this life

1 Corinthians 6:3 · KJV

There is no devil that can withstand me.

And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils

Mark 16:17 · KJV

There is no Satanic jurisdiction over me.

Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son

Colossians 1:13 · KJV

I am seated with Christ in heavenly places.

Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ

Ephesians 1:3 · KJV

No curse can prevail against me.

For when God made promise to Abraham, because he could swear by no greater, he sware by himself, Saying, Surely blessing I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee

Hebrews 6:13-14 · KJV

I manifest the reign of Christ on earth.

And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth

Matthew 28:18 · KJV

I do not yield to demonic influence.

But if ye have bitter envying and strife in your hearts, glory not, and lie not against the truth

James 3:14 · KJV

I disarm the enemy with the sword of God's Word.

Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds. Let the high praises of God be in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand

Psalm 149:5-6 · KJV
