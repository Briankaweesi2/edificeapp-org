Confessions on grace

Grace is not leniency. It is the unearned favour that empowers, elevates and promotes, and these confessions say so in the first person.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

My life is a continuous experience of bliss.

Moreover thou leddest them in the day by a cloudy pillar; and in the night by a pillar of fire, to give them light in the way wherein they should go

Nehemiah 9:12 · KJV

There is no struggle in my life.

For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his

Hebrews 4:10 · KJV

Every day is elevation for me.

Whereas thou hast been forsaken and hated, so that no man went through thee, I will make thee an eternal excellency, a joy of many generations

Isaiah 60:15 · KJV

The mercy of the Lord endures forever.

And when he had consulted with the people, he appointed singers unto the LORD, and that should praise the beauty of holiness, as they went out before the army, and to say, Praise the LORD; for his mercy endureth for ever

2 Chronicles 20:21 · KJV

I am exalted by the hand of God.

The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is the Lord’s doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes?

Matthew 21:42 · KJV

I shine with the glory of God.

The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple

Psalm 119:130 · KJV

The blessings of Abraham belong to me.

For all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen, unto the glory of God by us

2 Corinthians 1:20 · KJV

I live in the finished works of Christ.

Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen

Hebrews 11:1 · KJV

I walk in continuous promotion.

And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose

Romans 8:28 · KJV

I walk in divine honor and grace.

Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap

Galatians 6:7 · KJV

I testify the gospel of the grace of God.

But none of these things move me, neither count I my life dear unto myself, so that I might finish my course with joy, and the ministry, which I have received of the Lord Jesus, to testify the gospel of the grace of God

Acts 20:24 · KJV

Divine favour highlights everything I do.

And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man

Luke 2:52 · KJV
