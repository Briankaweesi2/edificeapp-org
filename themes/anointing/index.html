Confessions on the anointing

The anointing is power at work in and through the Christian. These confessions are said by someone who expects it to do something today.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

The miracle power of God lifts me.

When men are cast down, then thou shalt say, There is lifting up; and he shall save the humble person

Job 22:29 · KJV

There is no limitation in my life.

For the kingdom of God is not in word, but in power

1 Corinthians 4:20 · KJV

I am completely free from Satan's traps.

Then entered Satan into Judas surnamed Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve

Luke 22:3 · KJV

I am fortified with divine strength.

Be strong and quit yourselves like men, O ye Philistines, that ye be not servants unto the Hebrews, as they have been to you: quit yourselves like men, and fight

1 Samuel 4:9 · KJV

I walk conscious of the power within me.

But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth

Acts 1:8 · KJV

I experience the manifestation of God's power.

The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, be with you all. Amen

2 Corinthians 13:14 · KJV

My body is a vessel of divine glory.

But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us

2 Corinthians 4:7 · KJV

God's glory covers me completely.

Moreover thou leddest them in the day by a cloudy pillar; and in the night by a pillar of fire, to give them light in the way wherein they should go

Nehemiah 9:12 · KJV

I produce extraordinary results in all things.

Then he answered and spake unto me, saying, This is the word of the LORD unto Zerubbabel, saying, Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts

Zechariah 4:6 · KJV

The glory in me shines continuously.

To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles; which is Christ in you, the hope of glory

Colossians 1:27 · KJV

There is no bondage in my life.

That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved

Romans 10:9 · KJV

I manifest the mystery of godliness.

And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory

1 Timothy 3:16 · KJV
