Confessions on the new creation

The new creation is not a better version of the old one. These are confessions of what happened at the new birth, spoken in the present tense — because that is the tense they are true in.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

I have eternal life in Christ.

if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved

Romans 10:9 · KJV

I am a firstfruits of His creatures.

Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not

1 John 3:1 · KJV

As Jesus is, so am I in this world.

And so it is written, The first man Adam was made a living soul; the last Adam was made a quickening spirit

1 Corinthians 15:45 · KJV

I share in the divine nature of God.

Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust

2 Peter 1:4 · KJV

I no longer have the human life.

When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory

Colossians 3:4 · KJV

The glory of God is revealed in me.

But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit

1 Corinthians 6:17 · KJV

I manifest the beauty of God's craftsmanship.

For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them

Ephesians 2:10 · KJV

The life of God is at work in me.

And this is the record, that God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life

1 John 5:11-12 · KJV

I am conscious of my divine heritage.

These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life

1 John 5:13 · KJV

Eternal life is my present possession.

Even the mystery which hath been hid from ages and from generations, but now is made manifest to his saints

Colossians 1:26 · KJV

I share in the fullness of Him.

And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church, Which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all

Ephesians 1:22-23 · KJV

I possess the life of God in its fullness.

For he is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the middle wall of partition between us

Ephesians 2:14 · KJV
