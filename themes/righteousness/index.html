Confessions on righteousness

Righteousness is a gift, not a score. These confessions are for living free of guilt and condemnation because the standing was settled elsewhere.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

I am redeemed by the blood of Christ.

For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye doshew the Lord's death till he come

1 Corinthians 11:26 · KJV

My righteousness is the righteousness of Christ.

For if by one man's offence death reigned by one; much more they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ

Romans 5:17 · KJV

My justification is my present reality.

To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation

2 Corinthians 5:19 · KJV

God's nature of rightness is in me.

For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him

2 Corinthians 5:21 · KJV

God's record is settled in my heart.

And this is the record, that God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son

1 John 5:11 · KJV

I am free from every charge of sin.

But for us also, to whom it shall be imputed, if we believe on him that raised up Jesus our Lord from the dead; Who was delivered for our offences, and was raised again for our justification

Romans 4:24-25 · KJV

I walk in the righteousness of Christ.

Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends

John 15:13 · KJV

There is no falsehood in my life.

Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour: for we are members one of another

Ephesians 4:25 · KJV

I am blameless and harmless before God.

Do all things without murmurings and disputings: That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world

Philippians 2:14-15 · KJV

I am the righteousness of God in Christ.

Herein is our love made perfect, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment: because as he is, so are we in this world

1 John 4:17 · KJV

I am made the righteousness of God in Christ.

Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth

2 Timothy 2:15 · KJV

I am a minister of righteousness and truth.

I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority

1 Timothy 2:1-2 · KJV
