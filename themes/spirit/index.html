Confessions on the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is not an influence but a person, and he lives in you. These confessions are said from that fact rather than towards it.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

I manifest the presence of the Holy Spirit daily.

Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands

2 Timothy 1:6 · KJV

I walk in constant fellowship with the Spirit.

And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left

Isaiah 30:21 · KJV

I am born of the Spirit of God.

Verily I say unto you, All sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and blasphemies wherewith soever they shall blaspheme: But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost hath never forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal damnation

Mark 3:28-29 · KJV

I respond quickly to God's promptings.

And the Lord came, and stood, and called as at other times, Samuel, Samuel. Then Samuel answered, Speak; for thy servant heareth

1 Samuel 3:10 · KJV

I am completely yielded to the Holy Spirit.

For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost

2 Peter 1:21 · KJV

I manifest the joy of the Spirit daily.

Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full

John 16:24 · KJV

The full person of the Holy Spirit lives in me.

For he whom God hath sent speaketh the words of God: for God giveth not the Spirit by measure unto him

John 3:34 · KJV

I have the Spirit of God inside me.

Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?

1 Corinthians 3:16 · KJV

I am connected to a higher power.

That ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God

Colossians 1:10 · KJV

The Holy Spirit is the glory of my life.

But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his

Romans 8:9 · KJV

The presence of God is with me.

How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him

Acts 10:38 · KJV

God lives in me by His Spirit.

Wherefore if ye be dead with Christ from the rudiments of the world, why, as though living in the world, are ye subject to ordinances

Colossians 2:20 · KJV
