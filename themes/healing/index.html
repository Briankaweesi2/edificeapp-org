Confessions for healing

Healing is spoken before it is seen. Every confession here carries the verse it came from, so that what you are saying has something underneath it.

Twelve of them, and the scripture each one stands on

I speak words of healing and deliverance.

For he whom God hath sent speaketh the words of God: for God giveth not the Spirit by measure unto him

John 3:34 · KJV

Sickness and disease have no place in my life.

In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues; They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover

Mark 16:17-18 · KJV

I walk in supernatural health and strength.

Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands

2 Timothy 1:6 · KJV

I am whole in the Name of Jesus.

Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth

Philippians 2:9-10 · KJV

The life of Jesus reigns in my body.

The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?

1 Corinthians 10:16 · KJV

There is no mortality in my body.

And this is the record, that God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son

1 John 5:11 · KJV

The Word is health to all my flesh.

receive with meekness the engrafted word, which is able to save your souls

James 1:21 · KJV

Divine health is my daily experience.

What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own?

1 Corinthians 6:19 · KJV

I have received complete restoration.

And the king said, Is there not yet any of the house of Saul, that I may shew the kindness of God unto him?...

2 Samuel 9:3 · KJV

I walk in divine health every day.

The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly

John 10:10 · KJV

The law of life works in every fibre of my being.

For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free fromthe law of sin and death

Romans 8:2 · KJV

There is no incurable condition in my body.

And Jesus answering said unto them, Do ye not therefore err, because ye know not the scriptures, neither the power of God?

Mark 12:24 · KJV
