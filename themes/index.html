Themes

Choose what you are standing on.

A confession is scripture said back in the first person. These are the fifteen subjects Edifice draws from — pick the ones your life is asking about, and the app brings one a day from them.

New Creation Confessions on the new creation The new creation is not a better version of the old one. Healing Confessions for healing Healing is spoken before it is seen. Finances Confessions for finances Not affirmations about money. Prayer Confessions for prayer Prayer that knows what it is holding on to. Soul Winning Confessions for soul winning Soul winning begins with what you believe about your own mouth. The Holy Spirit Confessions on the Holy Spirit The Holy Spirit is not an influence but a person, and he lives in you. Authority & Dominion Confessions on authority and dominion Authority is exercised in words. Grace Confessions on grace Grace is not leniency. Righteousness Confessions on righteousness Righteousness is a gift, not a score. Wisdom Confessions for wisdom The mind renewed by the Word. Faith & Victory Confessions for faith and victory Faith is a shield and it is held up by speech. The Anointing Confessions on the anointing The anointing is power at work in and through the Christian. Love Confessions on love Love received before love expressed, in that order, because the second is impossible without the first. Purpose Confessions for purpose Purpose is not discovered by searching. Spiritual Growth Confessions for spiritual growth Growth is what a practice produces.
