Day 1 preview · Open the Word

Receive the living Word

  1. I receive God's Word with an open heart.
  2. I am born of God's living Word.
  3. I make room for Scripture in my day.
  4. I speak words that carry life and hope.
  5. I let God's truth take root in me.
Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.

1 Peter 1:23 · KJV

Let it take root.

God's Word is living seed. Receiving Scripture is more than collecting information: it begins a response of faith. Start this month with a receptive heart. Read slowly, believe what God says, and let that truth shape the words you speak today.

Put it into practice.

Read 1 Peter 1:23 aloud twice. Write one truth you want to carry into today.

A moment to reflect.

What would making room for God's Word look like in my ordinary day?

Keep your response in your Edifice journal.

A journey, one day at a time.

Begin by receiving the Word. Explore your identity in Christ, a renewed mind, love, the Holy Spirit, faith, purpose, and gratitude as the month unfolds.

Each day brings five related confessions with Scripture and a practical response. In the app, return to previous days as you go. Tomorrow’s content opens on its day.

Make room before November.

Download Edifice and find the Bible Month card below your main daily confession. Join the campaign waitlist in the app, then return on November 1 to begin.

Time to revisit.

After the 30-day journey, a ten-day review period gives you time to return to the words and passages you want to keep close.
