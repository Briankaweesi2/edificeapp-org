Daily Bible confessions

A few words.
A deeper foundation.

A confession in Edifice is a first-person declaration grounded in Scripture. It is an invitation to dwell on what the Bible says, not a promise that saying certain words will make life effortless.

Free members receive 3 daily confessions. Glory members receive 20 daily confessions and full confession-archive access.

A simple daily practice

01 / Read

Start with the verse.

Read the Scripture alongside your confession. Follow the reference into the wider passage to understand its context.

02 / Speak

Give the words room.

Speak the confession slowly, in your own quiet moment. Return to the verse when you want to think more deeply.

03 / Reflect

Carry it into today.

Ask what a faithful response might look like in an ordinary conversation, decision, or difficult moment.

An example · Faith

God has not given me a spirit of fear.

“For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.”

2 Timothy 1:7 · KJV
