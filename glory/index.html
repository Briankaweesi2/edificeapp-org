Before you subscribe

Where do I see the price?

Open Edifice and view the Glory offer. The purchase screen shows the current price, billing period, renewal terms, and any trial offer available to your account. Check those details before confirming.

Is Glory required to begin?

No. You can start with the free plan’s 3 daily confessions. Glory is an optional paid membership.

Need help with a purchase?

Visit Edifice support for help. For a Google Play purchase, you can also review your subscription in Google Play’s subscription settings.
