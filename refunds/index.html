Billing & customer care

Refund Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

We want billing for Edifice Glory to be clear. How you request a refund depends on where you made the purchase.

Purchases through Google Play

Request a refund through Google Play, using the Google account that made the purchase. Google reviews requests under its refund policies. If you cannot find the purchase or need help with access, email us and include your order details.

Purchases through Apple

Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple reviews requests under its refund policies.

Purchases on edificeapp.org

Email supernaturalbrian@gmail.com with the email address on your Edifice account, the order or receipt number, purchase date, and a short description of the issue. We review direct website requests individually and will reply as soon as we can. If a refund is approved, it will be sent to the original payment method where possible; your payment provider may need additional time to post it.

Subscription cancellations

A cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically request a refund for a charge already made. Cancel through the store where you subscribed. For a subscription purchased on edificeapp.org, email us at the address above. If a charge looks unfamiliar, appears twice, or you were billed after cancelling, contact us promptly so we can investigate.

Your rights

This page does not limit any refund or consumer rights that apply to you under local law. Store purchases remain subject to the relevant store’s procedures and policies.

Questions? Email supernaturalbrian@gmail.com.
