Account Management

Delete Your Account & Personal Data

You have the full right to delete your Edifice account and all associated personal data at any time.

How Account Deletion Works (2-Phase Process)

When you initiate an account deletion in the app (via POST /v1/account/deletion) or submit a web deletion request below, our system executes a secure two-phase deletion process:

1

Phase 1: Immediate Invalidation & Deactivation

All active authentication tokens, sessions, and device tokens are instantly revoked. Your account is immediately signed out on all devices and disabled from active use. Any published stories are immediately removed from public access.

2

Phase 2: ~7-Day Safety Cool-off Window

To protect members against accidental loss or unauthorized account hijacking, data is retained in a locked, inactive state for approximately 7 days. If you change your mind, signing back into the mobile app during this window will cancel the scheduled deletion.

3

Phase 3: Permanent & Irreversible Database Erasure

Once the 7-day window expires, the automated deletion sweep permanently deletes your account record, email, hashed password, profile, private journal entries, streak and practice history, saved goals, and preferences from our primary databases.

⚠️ Important Notice Regarding Data Erasure

What is completely erased: Your email address, profile, entire private journal, confession streak records, practice history, saved affirmations, and custom goals.

What is retained: An anonymized cryptographic hash receipt of your former user ID (to demonstrate regulatory deletion compliance without holding personal information) and unlinked transaction receipt tokens for accounting and tax obligations.

Option A: In-App Deletion (Instant Self-Serve)

If you have access to the Edifice app on your mobile device:

  1. Open Edifice.
  2. Navigate to the Me / Profile tab (or tap Settings).
  3. Select Settings → Account Deletion.
  4. Confirm your request. Your sessions will be terminated immediately and deletion will be scheduled.

Option B: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign into your device, you may submit your deletion request directly via this web page or by emailing our privacy team.

Submit Web Account Deletion Request

Email Support Instead
Your email app should now be open with the request filled in. Send it and we will confirm by reply. If nothing opened, email supernaturalbrian@gmail.com directly — that is the same route, and it is the one that counts.

Subscriptions & Billing

Please note that deleting your Edifice account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions billed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. To prevent future billing renewals, please manage or cancel your subscription directly in your Google Play or Apple ID account settings.
